What a roof replacement includes in Somerset County

A replacement starts with the old roof coming off completely. We do not lay a second layer over worn shingles.

Tearing off to the deck is the only way to see what is underneath. On Somerset County roofs that is where the real condition shows up: soft plywood near the eaves, old nail holes, and flashing that was never rebuilt the last time the roof was done.

More about roof replacement

A recent job, from a Google review in October 2026: Jeffrey Meltzer had us replace his roof. He wrote that our owner and team were professional and worked quickly and efficiently to finish the replacement in a timely manner.

Damaged decking is replaced before anything goes on top of it. We photograph the bare deck and talk it through with you before new plywood goes in.

Water protection goes on in layers, the same on every replacement our crew of six installs. Ice and water shield covers the valleys and the eaves, where ice dams and wind-driven rain push water up under shingles.

Synthetic underlayment covers the rest of the deck. Drip edge finishes the rakes and eaves so water runs into the gutters and not behind the fascia.

Penetrations are where leaks tend to start, so each one gets new flashing: plumbing vents, chimneys, skylights and the places where a roof meets a wall. A ridge ventilation upgrade lets attic air move out at the peak. Somerset County attics run hot in July and damp in January, so that detail matters as much as the shingles do.

The standard shingle is GAF Timberline HDZ. We also install Owens Corning Duration and CertainTeed Landmark, and we help you choose a color that suits your siding and trim. We are a GAF Certified contractor and an Owens Corning Preferred contractor, and our crew is insured.

When the last shingle is on, a magnetic sweep goes over the lawn, the planting beds and the driveway for stray nails. Then we do a final walkthrough with you and photograph the finished work. The 25-year workmanship warranty applies to the full replacement, and the manufacturer warranties on the materials sit alongside it.

Flat and low-slope sections on a house, such as a porch roof or an addition, use a different system than shingles. We install flat roofing systems, and we cover those sections at the same inspection as the main roof.

Not every roof needs this much work. If the damage is limited to one slope or one flashing detail, we will tell you a repair is the better call. Roof repair in Somerset, NJ is its own service, and the same inspection tells us which one fits your roof.

Permits come from your town's construction office, and we handle the paperwork. We have a $500 discount and low-rate financing available. Ask us for the terms when we inspect the roof.

Watch For These

Signs your Somerset County home needs roof replacement

  • Shingles curling, cracking or missing across more than one slope
  • Granules collecting in the gutters or at the foot of the downspouts
  • Brown stains on ceilings, or damp insulation and daylight showing in the attic
  • A leak that keeps coming back after it has been patched
  • A sagging ridge or soft spots in the roof deck
  • Flashing at the chimney or vents that has failed more than once

Our Roof Replacement Process

How roof replacement in Somerset County, NJ works with us

We inspect the roof and attic, put the scope and materials in writing, tear off to the deck and check it, then install the new roof and walk it with you. The 25-year workmanship warranty covers the finished job.

  1. Step : 01

    Call, then inspect the roof and attic

    Call (973) 938-2776 or send the form and we set a time. We go up on the roof, look at the attic from the inside, and use a drone where it helps. You get a photo report of what we found, so the scope matches the roof you actually have.

  2. Step : 02

    Written scope and materials

    You get an itemized scope that names the shingle line, underlayment, ventilation and flashing. We go over the $500 discount and low-rate financing here. There is no pressure to sign at the table.

  3. Step : 03

    The old roof comes off to the deck. We photograph the bare plywood, replace whatever is damaged, and tell you what was replaced.

  4. Step : 04

    Install and final walkthrough

    Ice and water shield, synthetic underlayment, drip edge, shingles, ridge ventilation and new flashing go on. A magnetic nail sweep follows, then a walkthrough with photos of the finished roof.
