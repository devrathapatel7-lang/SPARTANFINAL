Who we are

Spartan Roofing is a family owned roofing contractor at 220 Davidson Ave, Somerset, NJ 08873, licensed as New Jersey HIC 13VH12613900. This policy covers spartanroofing-nj.com and the forms we run on Facebook and Instagram.

What we collect

When you request a roof verdict or inspection, through our website or a Facebook or Instagram form, we collect what you give us:

  • Your name, phone number, email if you add it, and the property address and town
  • Your answers about the home and roof, such as whether you own it, its age, and what made you look at it

Our website also uses the Meta Pixel and similar tools, which record visits and form activity so we can measure our advertising and show it to the right homeowners.

How we use it

  • To call you about your request and arrange the inspection
  • To prepare and send your written roof verdict
  • To measure and improve our advertising

We call about what you asked for. We do not sell, rent or trade your information to anyone.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers that help us run the business, such as our email, form, and advertising platforms (including Meta), and only as needed for the purposes above. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your details while we are working with you and for a reasonable time afterward for our records, then delete them.
