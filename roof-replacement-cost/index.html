What moves the price

What decides the cost of a new roof

We at Spartan Roofing do not post a price for a roof replacement, because no two roofs need the same work. These are the things that move the number on your proposal.

The size of the roof

More roof means more material and more labor. We measure your roof at the free inspection with a drone and an on-roof assessment instead of guessing from the street.

Layers to tear off

Every replacement is a full tear-off to the deck, and we do not lay a second layer over worn shingles. Some houses have more than one old layer, so we open the edge, count, and show you.

Decking that needs replacing

Some roofs need a few sheets of decking and some need most of the deck, and you find out which when the shingles are off. We photograph the bare deck and talk it through with you before new plywood goes in.

The shingle you choose

Our standard is GAF Timberline HDZ. We also install Owens Corning Duration and CertainTeed Landmark. We are a GAF Certified contractor and an Owens Corning Preferred contractor.

Penetrations and flashing

Plumbing vents, chimneys, skylights and the places where a roof meets a wall each get new flashing. A roof with more of them takes more work.

Ventilation

A ridge ventilation upgrade lets attic air move out at the peak. If your attic runs hot in July and damp in January, that detail matters as much as the shingles do.

Insurance

When homeowners insurance pays part of it

It depends on the cause. Insurance may cover damage from storms, wind and fallen trees, but it usually does not cover old age, wear and tear or lack of maintenance.

We document the damage with photos and meet your adjuster, but the policyholder files the claim. Spartan Roofing does not waive, pay or absorb insurance deductibles.

Not sure your roof qualifies? We can inspect it and guide you, and you can read more about storm damage roof repair.

FAQs

Roof replacement cost: questions homeowners ask

What is the average cost of a roof replacement in NJ?

We do not publish an average, because the number on a proposal moves with the roof size, the layers to tear off, the decking, the shingle and the flashing; an average from another house tells you little about yours. The free inspection gives you a written, itemized price for your own roof.

How much should a 2000 sq ft roof cost?

Floor area is only part of it: the roof surface depends on the pitch and the shape of the roof, so we measure the roof itself with a drone and an on-roof assessment. After that you get a written price, with no obligation to hire us.

Should a 20-year-old roof be replaced?

Not always. Most asphalt shingle roofs in New Jersey last around 18 to 25 years, and we often replace them near the 20-year mark, especially once shingles start cracking, curling or losing granules. If yours is close to that age, have it inspected before leaks start, and we will tell you in writing whether a repair will hold.

What is the cheapest time of year to get a new roof?

Winter can sometimes be a little cheaper because demand is lower; the bigger saving is a roof installed right, whatever the month. We install roofs year-round, and a roof that is already leaking should not wait for a better season.
