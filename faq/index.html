Storm damage and insurance

Do you work with my insurance company after storm damage in Somerset County?

Yes. We tarp the roof, photograph the damage and work with your insurance company, but the insurer decides whether a claim is approved.

Does homeowners insurance cover a roof repair in Somerset County, NJ?

Can you tarp a leaking roof in Somerset?

Do you work with insurance companies on storm damage roof repair in Somerset, NJ?

What should I do first after a storm damages my roof in Somerset County?

Stay off the roof and keep everyone clear of any sagging ceiling. Move belongings away from drips, put a bucket under active leaks and take photos from the ground and from inside. Then call us at (973) 938-2776 so we can inspect and tarp if the roof needs covering.

Can you tarp my roof if it is leaking after a storm in Somerset?

How do you document storm damage for a claim in Somerset County, NJ?

We photograph each damaged slope, close-ups of creased, lifted or missing shingles, the flashing, the pipe boots and the attic. You get the photos to give your insurance company along with a clear description of the repair.

Does storm damage in Somerset County, NJ always mean a full roof replacement?

No. Many storm repairs involve a handful of shingles, a section of ridge cap or a flashing detail, and replacement only makes sense when damage is spread across the roof or the deck is compromised.

Can you inspect my roof after a storm in Somerset County?

Yes. We photograph the damage, tarp the roof if water is getting in and work with your insurance company, but the insurer decides whether a claim is approved.

Why does my flat roof leak after storms in Somerset?

Storms push water to the weak points: open seams, loose flashing at a wall, cracked pipe boots and drains blocked with leaves, which let water pool against the membrane. A leak often shows up in a ceiling several feet from where the water gets in, so we trace it on the roof instead of treating the stain.

Skylights

Can a skylight be installed on an existing roof in Somerset, NJ?

Yes, if the shingles around the spot have years left, we install the skylight and flash it into what is there. If the roof is close to replacement, we will tell you it is smarter to do the skylight with the new roof.

Do skylights leak, and how do you keep them dry in Somerset County?

A skylight leaks when its flashing is wrong or has aged out. To keep the odds low, we frame the opening to the rafters, put ice and water shield around it and layer the flashing with the shingles.

Can you replace a leaking skylight in Somerset County?

Chimneys and flashing

Why is my chimney still leaking in Somerset County after it was caulked?

Caulk is a surface seal, and chimneys move, so after a season or two the bead shrinks, cracks and pulls from the brick. A lasting fix replaces the metal in layers, with step flashing under the shingles and counter flashing over it, so the water is shed by overlap and sealant is only a backup.

Can chimney flashing be replaced without replacing the whole roof in Somerset, NJ?

Yes, if the shingles around the chimney are in decent shape, we remove the old flashing, replace the shingles beside the chimney, rebuild the flashing and tie it back into the existing roof. If the roof is near the end of its life, we tell you that at the inspection so you can plan.

Do you repair the chimney crown and mortar near Somerset?

Yes, we repair chimney crowns, tuckpoint mortar joints, install caps and apply a waterproof masonry sealant as part of a chimney repair. We do not sweep chimneys.

How do I know the leak is the flashing and not the chimney itself in Somerset County?

We start in the attic, where stains and wet wood along the sides and base of the chimney point to the flashing, while stains lower on the brick or inside the flue point somewhere else. We then match what we saw inside with the roof outside, which keeps us from repairing the wrong spot.

Siding, soffit and fascia

Should I choose vinyl or James Hardie siding for my home in Somerset County?

It depends on the house, the look you want and the budget. Vinyl is lighter, colored all the way through and easier to install over a wide range of walls, while James Hardie fiber cement is a denser board that holds paint and sharp lines for a painted-wood look. We explain the tradeoffs for your house so the choice is yours.

Is house wrap really necessary under new siding in Somerset?

Yes, because wind pushes some water behind the siding, and house wrap is the second layer that catches it and sends it back out. We install it on every siding job and flash windows, doors and roof joints over it.

Roof repair and leaks

What causes a roof to leak in Somerset, NJ?

Most leaks come from a detail: a cracked pipe boot, failed flashing at a chimney or wall, a worn valley, a lifted ridge cap, a dried-out skylight seal, or wind-lifted shingles. Water travels along the deck before it shows inside, so we trace it from the attic and from the roof.

Will repaired shingles match the rest of my roof in Somerset County, NJ?

We use the closest available match to your existing shingles. A new shingle can look a little different at first, and the difference softens as it weathers.

My Hillsborough roof has been repaired several times and still leaks. What now?

On a Hillsborough roof we trace the leak from the ceiling stain back to the source with an on-roof inspection and, when the attic is accessible, a look from inside. If the roof is past patching, we explain why and show you the photos before you decide.
