Chimneys and flashing

Why is my chimney still leaking in Somerset County after it was caulked?

Caulk is a surface seal, and chimneys move. After a season or two the bead shrinks, cracks and pulls from the brick, and water gets behind it again. A lasting fix replaces the metal in layers, with step flashing under the shingles and counter flashing over it, so the water is shed by overlap and sealant is only a backup.

Can chimney flashing be replaced without replacing the whole roof in Somerset, NJ?

Yes. If the shingles around the chimney are in decent shape, we can remove the old flashing, replace the shingles beside the chimney, rebuild the flashing and tie it back into the existing roof. If the roof is near the end of its life, we tell you that at the inspection so you can plan, not so we can sell you something.

Do you repair the chimney crown and mortar near Somerset?

Yes. We repair chimney crowns, tuckpoint mortar joints, install caps and apply a waterproof masonry sealant as part of a chimney repair. We do not sweep chimneys.

How do I know the leak is the flashing and not the chimney itself in Somerset County?

We start in the attic. Stains and wet wood along the sides and base of the chimney point to the flashing, while stains lower on the brick or inside the flue point somewhere else. We then match what we saw inside with the roof outside, which keeps us from repairing the wrong spot.

What is a chimney cricket, and does my home in Somerset need one?

A cricket is a small peaked saddle built behind a chimney on the uphill side. It splits the water running down the roof so it goes around the chimney instead of piling up against it. Wide chimneys usually need one. We check your roof pitch and the chimney width and tell you if it is worth adding.

Roof repair and leaks

Should I repair or replace my roof in Somerset, NJ?

It depends on how widespread the damage is. A few lifted shingles or one failed flashing is a repair. Shingles failing across the roof, a soft deck, or leaks that return after patching point to replacement. We inspect first and tell you which applies, with photos.

What causes a roof to leak in Somerset, NJ?

Most leaks come from a detail: a cracked pipe boot, failed flashing at a chimney or wall, a worn valley, a lifted ridge cap, a dried-out skylight seal, or wind-lifted shingles. Water also travels along the deck before it shows up inside, so the stain is rarely right under the entry point. We trace it from the attic and from the roof.

Can you repair my roof or do I need a replacement in Somerset County?

If the damage is local, a repair is usually the sensible choice. If shingles are failing across the roof, the deck is soft in several places, or earlier patches keep leaking, replacement makes more sense. You get the honest answer after the inspection, with photos, and replacement is quoted only if it is warranted.

Will repaired shingles match the rest of my roof?
