Storm damage repair for Somerset County roofs

We repair storm damage on Somerset County roofs by checking the whole roof and attic, tarping where water gets in, photographing the damage and then repairing or replacing. Roofs in Somerset County take wind from thunderstorms and nor'easters, rain that arrives sideways, falling limbs, and snow and ice loads in winter. Each one leaves different marks: wind creases and lifts shingles and can pull ridge caps loose.

Falling limbs puncture shingles and sometimes the deck. Ice backs water up under the eaves.

More about storm damage roof repair

A recent job, from a Google review by Nesibe Cimen: a storm damaged her roof and it started leaking, with more rainy days on the way. We came out quickly and completed the repair before the next rain.

We start by looking at the whole roof, not only the spot you noticed. We check the attic for wet insulation and stained sheathing, then the roof for lifted, creased or missing shingles, bent flashing and cracked pipe boots.

If water is getting in, we tarp. The tarp keeps the attic and ceilings from taking more water until the permanent repair is done.

Then we photograph each damaged slope, with close-ups of creased or missing shingles, the flashing, the pipe boots and the attic. You can hand that record to your insurance company, and we are glad to talk through the damage with your adjuster.

You file the claim, and the insurance company decides what it covers. We cannot promise an outcome.

Repairs follow the insurance conversation or run alongside it, depending on how much water is getting in. Wind damage usually means replacing shingles in the affected area, rebuilding flashing and replacing ridge caps, and where a limb has gone through the roof, damaged decking is replaced before new shingles go over it.

When the damage calls for a full roof, the job is the same as any other replacement we do: tear-off to the deck, ice and water shield in valleys and eaves, synthetic underlayment, drip edge, new flashing and a magnetic nail sweep. The 25-year workmanship warranty applies to full replacements, and the shingle makers back their materials with their own warranties.

We install GAF Timberline HDZ as the standard shingle, along with Owens Corning Duration and CertainTeed Landmark. We are a GAF Certified contractor and an Owens Corning Preferred contractor, and our crew is insured.

If the storm damage turns out to be limited, roof repair in Somerset, NJ may be all you need, and we will say so after the inspection. If it is not, you get the full picture with photos before you decide anything.

Watch For These

Signs your Somerset County home needs storm damage roof repair

  • Shingles creased, lifted or missing after a windstorm
  • Granules, pieces of shingle or a branch on the roof or around the house after a storm
  • A new stain on a ceiling or a drip in the attic after heavy rain
  • Bent, lifted or missing flashing at a chimney, vent or wall
  • Ridge caps loose or gone along the peak
  • Ice backed up at the eaves, with water showing inside the wall below

FAQs

Storm damage roof repair in Somerset County, NJ: frequently asked questions

Do you work with insurance companies on storm damage roof repair in Somerset, NJ?

Yes. We photograph the damage, scope the repair and talk it through with your adjuster, but you file the claim and your insurance company decides what it covers.

Will my insurance company approve my storm damage claim in Somerset County, NJ?

We cannot promise that, because approval is your insurer's decision. What we can do is make sure the damage is photographed and the repair is scoped accurately, so the claim is based on a clear record.

What should I do first after a storm damages my roof in Somerset County?

Stay off the roof and keep everyone clear of any sagging ceiling. Move belongings away from drips, put a bucket under active leaks and take photos from the ground and from inside. Then call us at (973) 938-2776 so we can inspect and tarp if the roof needs covering.

Can you tarp my roof if it is leaking after a storm in Somerset?

Yes. If water is getting in, we tarp the damaged area to protect the attic and ceilings until the permanent repair is done.

How do you document storm damage for a claim in Somerset County, NJ?

We photograph each damaged slope, close-ups of creased, lifted or missing shingles, the flashing, the pipe boots and the attic. You get the photos to give your insurance company along with a clear description of the repair.

Does storm damage in Somerset County, NJ always mean a full roof replacement?

No. Many storm repairs involve a handful of shingles, a section of ridge cap or a flashing detail, and replacement only makes sense when damage is spread across the roof or the deck is compromised.

Is a storm damage roof inspection free in Somerset, NJ?

Yes, it is free and there is no pressure to hire us. We tell you what we find and what the repair would involve.
