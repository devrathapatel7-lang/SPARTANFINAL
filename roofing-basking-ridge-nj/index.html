Local Conditions

Common roofing challenges in Basking Ridge, NJ

On Basking Ridge roofs we look for tree debris and shade, wear in the valleys of multi-plane roofs, skylight and chimney flashing, old decking on historic-era houses and gutters on big roofs. The town sits about 335 feet above sea level in the Raritan Valley.

  • Debris and shade from big trees The white oak by the Presbyterian church stood about 600 years before it was cut down in 2017 because of decay. Trees that old are a reminder that big trees age and drop limbs. Leaves and shade also shorten shingle life.
  • Valleys on multi-plane roofs Where two slopes meet, water concentrates, and in winter, snowmelt that refreezes in a valley can back up under the shingles. We put ice and water shield and fresh metal in each valley.
  • Skylights and chimneys More roof penetrations mean more flashing that can fail. We replace the flashing during a roof job.
  • Age on historic-era houses The downtown district was listed in 1974, and local landmarks date to 1768, 1809 and 1839. Older roofs may hide old decking, and we replace damaged boards.
  • Gutters on big roofs More roof area sends more water to the gutter. We install seamless aluminum gutters to handle it.

About the Area

All about Basking Ridge, NJ

Basking Ridge is a census-designated place inside Bernards Township, in the Somerset Hills region of Somerset County. It covers about 6.8 square miles and had around 2,700 housing units at the 2020 count.

Bernards Township was chartered in 1760. A roofer sees that range at a glance: a historic core, estates, and newer houses.

The downtown historic district was added to the state and national registers in 1974. Basking Ridge Presbyterian Church was built in 1839 in the Greek Revival style and enlarged in 1869.

More about roofing in Basking Ridge

The Brick Academy dates to 1809, and the Van Dorn Mill was first built in wood in 1768 and rebuilt in stone in 1843. If your home is in the historic district, ask the Bernards Township office whether the district affects the materials you choose, and share the answer with us before we order.

Large properties are part of the picture. The building that is now Bernards Township Municipal Hall began as an estate built in 1912 by Samuel Owen of Newark, and the Astors later owned it.

Houses on estates and large lots tend to come with complicated roofs: dormers, bays, long ridges, skylights and chimneys. Quoting one takes more time on the roof, which is why we use a drone and an on-roof assessment and send you the photos.

On any roof with trees nearby, we check for limb damage, moss, shade and clogged valleys, and we tell you which problems come from the roof and which come from what is above it.

Our crews reach Basking Ridge from Somerset on I-287 and CR 525. Replacements include tear-off to the deck, replacement of damaged decking, ice and water shield, synthetic underlayment, drip edge, ridge ventilation, new flashing at penetrations and a magnetic nail sweep.
