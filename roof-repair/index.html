Roof repairs in Somerset County that fix the cause

Most leaks start at a detail, not in the middle of a field of shingles. The usual causes are a cracked pipe boot, flashing that has pulled away from a chimney or a wall, a valley where the shingles have worn thin, a ridge cap that lifted in the wind, or a skylight whose seal has dried out, and each needs its own repair.

A recent job, from a Google review in September 2026: Alex Reyes needed a small roof repair after wind damage. Our owner handled it, and he wrote that we were professional, reasonable and prompt, and that he would use us again.

More about roof repair

The visit begins in the attic when we can get to it. Dark streaks on the sheathing, damp insulation and wet nail tips show where the water travels.

Then we go up and follow that line to the entry point. We use the same drone and on-roof assessment on a repair that we use for a replacement, and you get a photo report of what we find.

Shingle repairs are matched to your existing roof as closely as current stock allows. New shingles look a little different at first, and if your shingle line has been discontinued, we tell you the closest available match before we install it.

Flashing is where roofs meet chimneys, walls and skylights, and it fails more often than the shingles around it. Old caulk smeared over a gap is not a repair.

We replace or rebuild the flashing so water has a path off the roof. Skylights get a reseal or a flashing rebuild, depending on what is failing.

Valleys carry runoff from two slopes at once, and ridge caps take the wind at the peak. A worn valley gets rebuilt.

A lifted ridge cap gets replaced and fastened properly. Where a roof has been patched before, we look at what the earlier patch did and whether it is hiding the real problem.

Wind and storm damage repairs follow the same method with one addition. When the permanent repair has to wait, we tarp the damaged area and photograph the damage for your insurance company. The storm damage roof repair page for Somerset, NJ covers that process in full.

Sometimes the honest answer is replacement. If the shingles are failing across the whole roof, or the deck is soft in several places, paying for repair after repair does not make sense, and we show you why with photos.

A replacement is quoted separately. The 25-year workmanship warranty applies to full replacements only.

Watch For These

Signs your Somerset County home needs roof repair

  • A brown or yellow ring on a ceiling that grows after rain
  • Water stains on the wall beside a chimney or where a roof meets a wall
  • Shingles missing, lifted or creased after a windstorm
  • Granules or broken pieces of shingle in the gutters or on the ground below the roof edge
  • Daylight, damp insulation or dark streaks on the roof deck in the attic
  • A drip near a skylight or a plumbing vent

FAQs

Roof repair in Somerset County, NJ: frequently asked questions

What causes a roof to leak in Somerset, NJ?

Most leaks come from a detail: a cracked pipe boot, failed flashing at a chimney or wall, a worn valley, a lifted ridge cap, a dried-out skylight seal, or wind-lifted shingles. Water travels along the deck before it shows inside, so we trace it from the attic and from the roof.

Will repaired shingles match the rest of my roof?

We use the closest available match to your existing shingles. A new shingle can look a little different at first, and the difference softens as it weathers.

Does homeowners insurance cover a roof repair in Somerset County, NJ?

That depends on your policy and on what caused the damage. We photograph the damage so you have a record for your insurance company, but your insurer decides what it covers and we cannot promise that a claim will be approved.

Is there a warranty on a roof repair from Spartan Roofing?

Our 25-year workmanship warranty applies to full roof replacements. For a repair, ask us what applies to your job before you approve the work, and we will tell you plainly.

Can you tarp a leaking roof in Somerset?

Yes. We tarp a damaged area to keep water out of the attic and ceilings until the permanent repair. Call (973) 938-2776 to talk through your situation.
