FAQs

GAF certified roofer: frequently asked questions

What does GAF Certified mean?

GAF, the largest roofing manufacturer in North America, verifies a contractor's insurance, training and customer feedback before granting certification. Certified contractors are factory-trained to install GAF roofing systems correctly, which is what opens up GAF's enhanced warranty coverage.

Does a GAF Certified roofer cost extra?

No. Certification costs Spartan Roofing more in training, audits and insurance, but the warranty coverage comes with every GAF system we install. You get a stronger warranty at no premium.

What if a non-certified roofer installs GAF shingles?

You only get the basic limited material warranty, with no enhanced workmanship coverage from GAF. If something fails because of an install error, you are back to dealing with whoever installed it.
