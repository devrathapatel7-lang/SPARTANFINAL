What our roof inspection in Somerset County, NJ covers

Our inspection covers the roof only: the flashing, penetrations, valleys, edges, ventilation and shingles, checked by drone, on foot and from the attic. That is not the same as a home inspection, where an inspector looks at the whole house for a buyer.

We look at one thing, the roof, and we know what we are looking at. We have 15+ years of roofing experience, and the inspection is built around the places roofs tend to fail first: flashing, penetrations, valleys, edges and ventilation, before the shingles themselves.

More about roof inspection

A recent example, from a Google review by Martin Joyce: on a skylight call at his mother's home, our owner also inspected the roof and the other skylights and photographed a few issues. He recommended watching them for leaks and calling the original roofer about warranty repairs.

The drone goes up first. It gives us the full picture of every slope and every feature on the roof, such as chimneys, vents, skylights and dormers, and it records them in photos. That lets us spot patterns, like a run of lifted shingles on the weather side of the house, that are easy to miss from a ladder.

Then we assess the roof on foot where it is safe. We press on soft spots, check the flashing at the chimney, at the walls and around every pipe and vent, and look at the valleys and the drip edge along the eaves.

Sealant that has dried and cracked, nails that have backed out and shingles that are cupping or have lost their granules all show up here. If there is a skylight, we check its flashing as well.

In the attic we look at the underside of the roof deck. Dark stains point to a slow leak. Daylight where it should not be points to a gap.

Frost or damp insulation points to a ventilation problem, which can shorten the life of a roof that looks fine from the street. Not every attic can be entered, and we will tell you when we could not check one.

After a storm, the inspection also works as documentation. If wind, hail or a fallen branch has damaged the roof, we photograph it and, where it makes sense, tarp the roof to keep water out.

We work with your insurance company on the claim. We never promise a claim will be approved, because that is the insurer's decision.

The photo report is yours. It shows what we found, where and how bad it is, and which findings need action now and which can wait and be watched. If the answer is that the roof has years left, that is what the report says.

If it needs a repair, a section or a full replacement, we give you the options and the reasoning, and you choose. Our owner puts it plainly: he takes the time to inspect the roof, explain what he finds and help you pick the right solution for your home and budget.

There is no fee for any of this, and no obligation to hire us.

Watch For These

Signs your Somerset County home needs roof inspection

  • Shingles that are curling, cracked, missing or showing bare patches
  • Brown or dark stains on a ceiling, or damp spots on the underside of the roof deck
  • Granules from the shingles collecting at the bottom of your downspouts
  • Daylight visible through the roof boards from inside the attic
  • A roof that was damaged in a storm or by a fallen branch
  • A roof nobody has looked at since it was installed

FAQs

Roof inspection in Somerset County, NJ: frequently asked questions

Is a roof inspection really free in Somerset, NJ?

Yes. The inspection costs you nothing and there is no obligation to hire us afterward. You keep the photo report either way.

Will you push a full replacement after inspecting my Somerset County roof?

No. If the roof has years left, the report says so. If it needs a repair or a section replaced, that is what we recommend. A full replacement is on the table only when the roof calls for it, and we show you the photos that explain why.

How does a drone roof inspection work in Somerset?

The drone flies over the roof and photographs every slope and feature, including areas that are steep or not safe to walk. We pair it with an on-roof assessment and an attic check, so you get both the overview and the close-up.

Do you go into the attic during an inspection?

Yes, where we can. The underside of the roof deck shows stains, daylight and ventilation problems that you cannot see from above. If an attic has no access, we tell you what we could not check.

Can you inspect my roof after a storm in Somerset County?

Yes. We document the damage with photos, tarp the roof if water is getting in, and work with your insurance company. We do not promise a claim will be approved, because the insurer decides that.

What do I get after a roof inspection in Somerset, NJ?

A photo report with plain notes on what we found, where it is and how urgent it is. It is yours to keep, even if you get quotes from other roofers.

How often should a Somerset County homeowner have the roof inspected?

After any big storm, whenever you see a stain on a ceiling, and any time you have not looked at the roof in a while. There is no fixed number that fits every house, which is why the inspection is free to ask for.
