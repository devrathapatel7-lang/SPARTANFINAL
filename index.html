FAQs.

Looking for more info? Our most common questions are answered below.

In Central NJ, most roof repairs range between $300 and $1,500, depending on the issue. · Small repairs (flashing, pipe boots) → $150–$500 · Moderate repairs → $500–$1,500 · Major damage → $2,000+ We often see cases where a 'small leak' is actually a bigger issue underneath. That's why a proper inspection matters.
A reliable roofer should have: ✓ Proper licensing and insurance ✓ Strong local reviews ✓ Clear, detailed estimates ✓ A workmanship warranty ✓ Good communication In Central NJ, reputation matters — check reviews and recent projects. At Spartan Roofing, we focus on transparency, quality installation, and long-term results.
Winter can sometimes be slightly cheaper due to lower demand. However, the real value is choosing the right contractor, not the cheapest timing. A poorly installed roof will cost you much more long-term.
In NJ, the most popular and value-boosting colors are: · Charcoal · Pewter Gray · Barkwood · Weathered Wood These colors match most homes and improve curb appeal. We help homeowners choose colors that fit their house and neighborhood.
It depends on the cause. Insurance may cover damage from: · Storms · Wind · Fallen trees But it usually does NOT cover: · Old age · Wear and tear · Lack of maintenance We've helped many homeowners in Central NJ determine whether their issue qualifies for a claim. If you're not sure, we can inspect it and guide you.
In New Jersey, most asphalt shingle roofs last around 18–25 years depending on weather conditions and installation quality. We see a lot of wear from heat, heavy rain, and winter freeze-thaw cycles. We often replace roofs right around the 20-year mark, especially when shingles start cracking, curling, or losing granules. If your roof is getting close to this age, it's smart to have it inspected before leaks start.
The best time in New Jersey is typically spring through fall (April–October). That said — we install roofs year-round. The key is proper installation, not just timing. · Spring/Fall → ideal temperatures · Summer → faster installs but hotter conditions · Winter → still possible, but requires experienced crews If your roof is leaking, don't wait for the 'perfect season' — fix it before it gets worse.
