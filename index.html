How It Works

How a roof replacement or repair in Somerset County works with us

We give you a free inspection with a photo report, an itemized proposal, the tear-off and install, and a nail sweep, walkthrough and photos at the end. You know what comes next before it happens.

  1. Step : 01

    Call or send the form, then a free inspection

    Call (973) 938-2776 or send the form and we set a time. We check the roof with a drone and an on-roof assessment, and you get a photo report of what we found, including the spots that are fine.

  2. Step : 02

    Itemized proposal

    You get a written proposal that lists the work line by line, with a straight recommendation on repair or replacement. We answer questions before you sign anything.

  3. Step : 03

    Tear-off and install

    On a replacement we tear off to the deck, replace any damaged decking, and install ice and water shield, underlayment, drip edge, new flashing and ridge ventilation.

  4. Step : 04

    Walkthrough, nail sweep and photos

    We sweep the yard and driveway with a magnet for loose nails, walk the finished roof with you and send you photos of the finished work. After the job, questions go straight to our owner.

FAQs

Roofing questions from Somerset and Hunterdon County homeowners

Is the roof inspection really free in Somerset County, NJ?

Yes. We inspect the roof with a drone and an on-roof assessment, take photos, and explain what we see. You get a photo report with no cost and no pressure to hire us.

What does the 25-year warranty cover on a roof replacement?

The 25-year warranty is a workmanship warranty, and it covers full roof replacements by Spartan Roofing. Materials such as shingles carry the manufacturer's own warranty. A repair does not carry the 25-year workmanship warranty.

Which shingles do you install in Somerset, NJ?

Our standard is the GAF Timberline HDZ shingle. We also install Owens Corning Duration and CertainTeed Landmark. We will show you the options for your house during the inspection.

Do you work with my insurance company after storm damage in Somerset County?

Yes. We tarp the roof, document the damage with photos, and work with your insurance company. We cannot promise a claim will be approved, because that decision belongs to the insurer.

How do I ask about the discount or financing?

Ask us when you call or fill out the form. We will explain how each one applies to your roof once we have looked at it.

Do you serve Hunterdon County as well as Somerset County?

Yes. Most of our work is in Somerset County, and we also take jobs in Flemington and the surrounding area of Hunterdon County. Call (973) 938-2776 to confirm your address.

What is included when you replace a roof in Somerset County?

We tear off to the deck, replace any damaged decking, and install ice and water shield in the valleys and eaves, synthetic underlayment, drip edge, ridge ventilation and new flashing at penetrations. We finish with a magnetic nail sweep and a walkthrough.
