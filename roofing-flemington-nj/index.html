Local Conditions

Common roofing challenges in Flemington, NJ

A roof in Flemington takes about 51 inches of precipitation a year over roughly 126 wet days, and the temperature swings hard. The five problems below come from that mix.

  • Ice at the eaves February is the snowiest month here. When attic heat melts snow on the upper roof and the water refreezes at the eave, it backs up under the shingles. Ice and water shield and proper ventilation stop it.
  • Heat and cold cycling Highs have reached 108 degrees in August and lows minus 18 in winter. That range works shingles loose and cracks old sealant around chimneys and vent pipes.
  • Old layers on old decking In the older blocks near the historic district, a roof can carry several layers of shingle over aged boards. We tear off to the deck and replace any damaged decking before the new roof goes on.
  • Flat sections on older buildings Downtown has porches, additions and commercial buildings with low-slope roofs. We install flat roofing systems, including TPO, and rebuild the flashing where they meet a wall.
  • Skylight and chimney leaks Penetrations are where a roof usually fails first. We replace the flashing instead of smearing new sealant over the old.

If you see a stain on a ceiling or granules in the gutter, call for a free roof inspection and we will tell you what we find.

About the Area

All about Flemington, NJ

Flemington has been the Hunterdon County seat since 1785. The courthouse stands on Main Street, and the borough, which became its own municipality in 1910, is completely surrounded by Raritan Township.

That layout matters when you pull a permit: the borough and the township are separate governments with separate offices. Before we start, we confirm which one covers your address, and we handle the paperwork once we know.

The borough has a National Register historic district, and by 1980 most of the borough was on the state register. Landmarks give a sense of the age: the Union Hotel building on Main Street dates to 1877, and Fleming Castle on Bonnell Street is described as the first house in town.

Flemington is a borough that sits entirely inside Raritan Township, so the two are easy to confuse. The right construction office depends on where the lot is. We check that before we price a permit and we handle the paperwork for the office that applies.
